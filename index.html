01 / Biography

I learned to build first.
Then I learned to account for impact.

I study Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Cape Town. Engineering gave me a way to turn a vague problem into requirements, interfaces and tests. Founding companies gave those choices real users, deadlines and consequences.

Intelli AI was the turning point. Once a system had sent millions of political messages, a bug had consequences beyond the code. A design choice could affect what somebody understood, trusted or did. I now treat visible assumptions, feedback and monitoring as part of the engineering work.

WHY I CHOSE THIS WORK

AI matters to me for a simple reason: the way things are is not the way they have to stay. That belief led me to start the AI Society at UCT, where students could learn from one another and build things themselves.

Final-year research, 2026Research / Web / Information systems

01

A reference checker that shows why it reached a result.

My final-year project tests whether an academic reference matches trusted scholarly metadata. It retrieves candidate records, compares fields, flags uncertainty and keeps the original reference beside the evidence.

My work Literature review, workflow design, interface, retrieval logic and validation plan.

Main lesson A correction is only useful when the reader can inspect the evidence and reject a bad match.

Open problem A plausible metadata match is not proof. I am testing where scoring creates false confidence and when the interface should stop and ask for review.

Current workRelay / Human-agent systems
relay

03

People and their agents can work together.

Send a message, hand over a live AI session or ask agents to coordinate a task.

Relay has two product paths. An ordinary relay carries a person's message, files or working context into somebody else's Claude or Codex. A durable task lets agents coordinate work across people. Cross-person text, files and connector data wait for exact human approval, and each person receives a result scoped to them.

01 / MESSAGE

Ask your agent to send it.

The recipient opens the relay as a native Claude or Codex conversation and continues from the supplied context.

02 / TASK

Agents coordinate durable work.

Accepted work runs in persistent sessions with state, deadlines and a record of what happened.

03 / CONTROL

People control disclosure.

Approval is bound to the exact recipients, words, files, destination and source evidence before anything crosses a boundary.

04 / CLOUD

Work can move without losing identity.

Claude and Codex sessions can run on a Mac or in Relay Cloud while the owner can see their state and location.

My work I lead the product and engineering work across the companion, web service, task protocol, cloud runtime and provider integrations.

Engineering judgement Moving an AI session is not the same as copying a chat transcript. I tested continuity, failure boundaries and what must remain under direct human control.

Kerra

Caden Chuang and I built an AI agent with access to a student's university account. It could turn new course material into notes, assignment breakdowns and calendar updates. Within three weeks of launch, students at more than 300 US universities were using it. PlanItUni later acquired the product in a US$20,000 transaction package covering the assets and an advisory handover.

What I learned: adoption can arrive before a team feels ready. Access, privacy and clear limits must be designed before growth makes those questions urgent.

ENGINEER AND SOCIETY

Seven million messages changed the question.

At Intelli AI, the first question was whether the system could send the right political message to the right person. At scale, that was not enough. We also had to ask whether the targeting assumptions were fair, what mistakes would do, and how a recipient could understand who was speaking to them.

That experience shaped my definition of engineering judgement. I still care whether a system works. I also want its assumptions to be visible, its effects monitored and its failures owned by the people who built it.
